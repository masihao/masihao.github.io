About Me

I am a Hedrick Math Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at UCLA, mentored by Zhouli Xu.

I obtained my Ph.D. in mathematics from University of Notre Dame in 2025, advised by Mark Behrens.

I am mainly interested in computational homotopy theory, especially in equivariant and chromatic aspects, with the tools of motivic homotopy theory and synthetic spectra.

My email address is masihao.math@gm**l.com.
